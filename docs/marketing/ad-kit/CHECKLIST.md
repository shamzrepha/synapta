# Before you spend on ads

## Claims: every line must be true on the day it runs
| Say | True today? | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| Notes, scans or slides become lessons, flashcards and quizzes | Yes (landing page copy, features exist) | landing page "How it works" |
| A tutor | Yes | the tutor in the app |
| Tap any word for an explanation | Yes, shown in clip-1 | reader |
| Textbook-style pages and readable tables | Yes, shown in screens | reader |
| 10-minute focus sprints | Yes, shown in clip-2 | Sprout menu |
| Name your Sprout, earn XP, dress it | Yes (rename on the pet page, wardrobe bought with XP in the shop) | pet page and shop |
| "Start free" | The landing page says it. Confirm the free tier still exists on the day. | signup flow |
| Anything about marks, grades, ranking, "#1", numbers of students | **No: do not use** until you can prove it | |

Do not say the app "improves grades", "cures" anything or helps a medical condition. For the focus sprint say only what it is: a 10-minute timer with Sprout.

## Pictures you still need from your own phone (I could not sign in)
Use a test account with a made-up name. Hide your email and any real student names.
1. **Home / Today** screen on a phone, with one or two items showing.
2. A **class map** or the topic list of one class.
3. A **quiz or flashcard** screen mid-question.
4. **Sprout's wardrobe** in the shop (hat, glasses, shoes) and the pet page with its name.
Frame each as a phone-sized screenshot (about 9:19.5), no notification bar clutter.

## Platform and legal
- Add an AI-content label wherever the platform asks. Sprout is a cartoon, but generated clips from Flow or Vids may carry Google's invisible watermark and some platforms want disclosure.
- Music: use only the Vids library or a licensed track.
- Do not use real students' faces or voices without written permission. Under-18s need a parent's consent.
- Check each platform's ad rules for education and for under-18 audiences before you boost anything.
- Keep a copy of the unedited Flow and recording files.

## Make it measurable
- One link per ad with a tail, for example `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_campaign=hookB`. Confirm your analytics reads it before you rely on it.
- Run the three hooks for the same budget for 2 to 3 days. Keep the one with the most sign-ups, not the most views.
- Look at where people stop watching. If they leave in the first 3 seconds, change the first line, not the ending.

## Quality
- Captions on, big, high contrast. No flashing faster than 3 times a second (Sprout's backflip is fine).
- Test on a real phone with the sound off.
- Make sure no dev badge, email address or test-account detail is visible in any frame.

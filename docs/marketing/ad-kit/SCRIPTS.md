# Scripts: Sprout introduces SunrootLearn

**Voice:** Sprout is a cheerful, slightly dramatic study buddy. Short sentences. A little funny, never preachy. Speak to one person ("you"), not to a crowd.
**Format:** vertical 9:16, captions always on (most people watch with the sound off). Pace: about 2.5 words per second.
**Link on the end card:** `sunrootlearn.vercel.app` (swap for your own domain if you have one). Add `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_campaign=hookB` style tails so you can tell which ad worked.
**Claims used:** only things the app does today: notes, scans and slides become lessons, flashcards and quizzes; a tutor; tap any word for an explanation; textbook-style pages; 10-minute focus sprints; Sprout that you name and dress with XP. No numbers, no testimonials, no "#1".

Sources of pictures: **[F]** = generate in Flow (see FLOW-PROMPTS.md), **[C]** = the real clip, **[S]** = a screenshot from `screens/`.

## Cut-downs and variations to test later

- Same ad with Sprout in the wizard hat for exam-season posts, or the crown for results week.

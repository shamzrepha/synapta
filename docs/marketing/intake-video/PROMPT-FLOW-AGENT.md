# Prompt for an agent using Google Flow (or any video tool). Copy everything below the line.

---

Make a 40 to 45 second vertical (9:16) welcome video for **SunrootLearn**, a study app, aimed at students who have just been admitted to university. Tone: warm, funny, celebratory. The host is **Sprout**, a small round blue robot pet. Audience speaks English.

## Tools and rules
- Preferred: Google Flow for the animated shots (Frames to Video or Ingredients, about 8 s per clip), Google Vids or any editor (CapCut, DaVinci, Canva) to assemble. If Flow is unavailable, use any text-to-video or image-to-video tool, or animate the stills with motion graphics in the editor.
- **Never let the AI draw text or app screens.** Use the real screenshots supplied and add all words in the editor.
- One voiceover for the whole video: a robot voice (text-to-speech with a robot or vocoder effect, higher pitch). Do not use each clip's own audio.
- Upload as references: the Sprout images in `docs/marketing/ad-kit/sprout/` (use `02-sprout-neutral.png` as the main one) and the screenshots in `docs/marketing/intake-video/assets/`.
- Colours: deep blue gradient background, white text, Sprout blue and teal. Gold only for rewards (XP, crown, confetti).

## Sprout (paste in every Flow prompt)
> Sprout: a small, round, friendly robot-pet. Smooth blue egg-shaped body, a white rounded face screen with two big glossy black eyes with a white highlight, pink blush circles, a small smile, tiny stubby arms, round feet, and one green sprout with two teal leaves on top of its head. Clean, bright, flat-shaded 3D cartoon style like a premium mobile-game mascot. Soft lighting, saturated colours, no outlines. Match the reference image exactly.

## Shots, voiceover and on-screen words
1. **0-4 s.** Sprout, wearing a black graduation cap, pops up in a burst of confetti and waves both arms. VO: "Beep boop! Congratulations! You got admitted!" Text: *Congratulations!*
2. **4-7 s.** Expanding light rings behind Sprout, who hops happily. VO: "Welcome to the next chapter of your life." Text: *Welcome to the next chapter.*
3. **7-10 s.** Sprout in the middle, waving. VO: "I'm Sprout. Your brand new study buddy." Text: *I'm Sprout.*
4. **10-15 s.** Sprout looks around at floating cards and a growing pile of paper. VO: "First year is a lot. New courses. New lecturers. So many notes." Text: *First year is a lot.*, then the three card labels.
5. **15-22 s.** Phone slides up showing `phone-lesson.png`. File chips (Lecture notes, Slides, Photo of the board) fly into it; Lessons, Flashcards, Quizzes pop out. VO: "Drop in your notes, your slides, even a photo of the board. I turn them into lessons, flashcards and quizzes." Text: *Notes in. Lessons out.*
6. **22-27 s.** Screen recording of the real tap-a-word moment if you have it (`docs/marketing/ad-kit/clips/clip-1*.mp4`), otherwise `phone-lesson.png` then `phone-popup.png` with Sprout running in with a magnifying glass and hauling the card up with a rope. VO: "Stuck on a word? Tap it. I'll run over and pull the answer up for you." Text: *Tap any word.*
7. **27-31 s.** `phone-focus.png`; Sprout wearing headphones beside a "10-minute sprint" tag. VO: "Can't focus? Start a ten minute sprint. I'll keep watch." Text: *Stay focused.*
8. **31-36 s.** Gold "+50 XP" and "Day 3 streak" badges; Sprout puts on cap, crown, sunglasses, sneakers and cape, then does a backflip. VO: "Earn XP. Keep your streak. And yes, you can dress me up!" Text: *Earn XP. Keep your streak.*
9. **36-43 s.** Second confetti burst, SunrootLearn logo (`assets/logo.svg`), Sprout waving, button "Start free today", link `sunrootlearn.vercel.app`. VO: "Congratulations again. Start free today. SunrootLearn. Let's go!"

## Finishing
- Captions on for every spoken word, big (about 52 px at 1080 wide), high contrast, inside the safe area (keep the top 8% and bottom 12% free of anything but the caption).
- Music: soft, upbeat, licensed or generated; lower it under the voice.
- Export 1080x1920 H.264. Also export 1:1 and 16:9 if time allows.
- No real faces, no school logos, no brand names, no claims about grades or student numbers. Label AI-generated clips where the platform asks.
- Deliver the MP4, the project file, and a note listing which tool made each shot.

# Script: "Welcome to uni" (new intake, about 43 s, vertical 9:16)

**Audience:** students who have just been admitted. English speakers, so no language angle.
**Voice:** Sprout, a cheerful robot (a processed text-to-speech voice: pitch up, light buzz and grit). Short sentences, one idea each.
**Look:** blue brand, motion graphics, confetti, Sprout in a graduation cap. Gold only for rewards (XP, crown, confetti).
**Claims:** only things the app does today. No grades, no numbers, no rankings.

# SunRootLearn: motion-graphic video brief (give this whole file to ChatGPT)

## 0. Your job
You are a senior creative director and scriptwriter for short product videos. Using ONLY the facts in this file, write a 60-second motion-graphic video script that makes new university students (fresh intakes) in Nigeria want to try SunRootLearn.

The video will be built from animated vector graphics, icons, charts, type and a mascot. There are no actors and no screen recordings, so write visuals that can be made with shapes and motion. Do not invent features, numbers, prices, awards or testimonials. If a fact is not in this file, do not claim it.

## 1. Deliverables (in this order)
1. One-line concept and the emotional arc (hook, problem, turn, proof, payoff, call to action).
2. Three alternative hooks for the first 3 seconds.
3. SCENE-BY-SCENE TABLE, total exactly 60 seconds. Columns: scene no., start-end time, exact voiceover words, exact on-screen text (max 7 words), visual direction (what moves and how, in shapes/icons/type), transition to next scene, sound cue.
4. Voiceover as one clean block for text-to-speech, 130 to 150 words, with pauses marked.
5. At least 8 features shown in a story order (not a list). Suggested story: the night before a test, a pile of notes, one upload, clean notes appear, Sprout reads along, a quiz, a mistake becomes a lesson, a countdown to the exam, a pass.
6. A 5-second end card: logo mark, tagline, "sunrootlearn.com", "Free to start", one call to action.
7. Music and sound direction (mood, tempo, 3 sound effects) and a note on what must also work with the sound off: every key message must appear as on-screen text.
8. Subtitle file: the voiceover split into subtitle lines with timecodes.
9. Two cut-downs of the same script: 30 seconds and 15 seconds, saying which scenes to keep.
10. A list of anything in your script I should double-check for accuracy before publishing.
11. Notes for 9:16 vertical (TikTok, Reels, WhatsApp Status): how each scene reflows.

Banned claims unless I supply a number: "guaranteed to pass", "number one", "used by thousands", any exam-score promise, any price.

## 2. Audience and tone
New intakes: excited, nervous, short of money and data, often studying at night on a phone, facing big lecture notes and CBT and theory exams for the first time. Tone: warm, confident, a little playful, never childish, never preachy, never mocking students or any school. Nigerian English with light Pidgin touches is welcome ("no stress", "e go make sense") but keep it clear for any student.

## 3. Brand and design rules
- Name: Sunroot (also written SunRootLearn, Sunrootlearn). Site: sunrootlearn.com. Tagline: "Understand it. Build it. Prove it."
- Colour: blue leads (actions, links, knowledge). Gold ONLY for rewards (XP, streaks, badges), never for a button. Green means correct or saved. Coral or red means needs attention, always with an icon or words. Dark navy backgrounds with off-white type work well.
- Type: Instrument Sans for body, Instrument Serif italic for one emphasised word per headline, IBM Plex Mono (uppercase, widely spaced) for small labels and numbers. Small crisp corners, shadows only on floating layers.
- Mascot: Sprout, a friendly plant-sprout character. He guides, he does not dominate. He can read along, cheer, think, and wear outfits from the XP shop.
- Motion: calm and purposeful, one easing curve, no strobing, nothing that flashes more than 3 times a second.
- There are 18 colour themes (including pink, purple and green ones); the video may show the theme changing as a delight moment.

## 4. What SunRootLearn is
SunRootLearn (brand "Sunroot", mascot "Sprout") turns a student's own material (a PDF, a photo of handwritten notes, a scan, lecture slides) into clean study notes, interactive lessons, quizzes, flashcards, written practice and a read-aloud lesson. It then keeps bringing the student back at the right time so it sticks. It also has classes for lecturers, study with friends, virtual science labs, and rewards.

The core loop to show: Capture (add your material) → Understand (notes, tutor, read-along) → Retrieve (quiz, flashcards, write or say the answer) → Correct (mistakes become lessons) → Revisit (spaced review before the exam).

Promise in one line: "Your own notes become a complete study plan you can use on a phone."

## 5. Complete feature list (all are live)

### Get your material in
- **Study notes**: upload a PDF, photo or scan and get clean, structured notes you can refine. Works with handwritten photos.
- **Snap your homework**: photograph a problem and the AI explains it step by step.
- **AI Notebook**: chat with your own documents and get answers with sources.
- **My Worlds**: upload your notes, merge them into one library, and learn them like a game with a map of ideas.
- **Add material button**: one tap from every screen (a big plus in the middle of the phone's bottom bar).

### Understand it
- **AI tutor**: ask anything in your own words, by typing or by voice. Answers in English, Pidgin (Naijá), Yorùbá, Igbo, Hausa, French, Arabic and Swahili.
- **Voice tutor**: talk to the AI hands-free.
- **Read-along**: a natural voice reads the lesson while the current word highlights and Sprout moves along the words. The notes look like a real notebook page with ruled lines and page turns. Pace can be chosen.
- **Explain it like I'm 12**: switch any explanation between simple and lecturer level.
- **Mind-map maker**: a mind map from any lesson or your notes.
- **Worked solutions**: past questions explained as a numbered, textbook-style solution with proper maths, and "where students lose marks" warnings.

### Practise it
- **Daily 5-minute challenge**: five questions a day; the result shows instantly.
- **Quizzes** on every topic from a large question bank, instant right-or-wrong feedback.
- **Past questions** by topic: objective (CBT, like JAMB) and theory.
- **Exam sprint**: pick CBT, theory or mixed, pick topics, how many questions and how long (for example a 30-minute sprint). Questions can be read aloud. Answer by tapping, typing, speaking, or photographing your paper work; the AI marks it. The clock stops while marking. Results by topic show weak spots.
- **Written practice**: answer in your own words and get marked against a marking guide, with feedback on what to improve.
- **Past-paper mode**: timed papers, AI-marked, with a weak-topic report.
- **How am I doing?**: finds what you are stuck on from your wrong answers and gives a short plan.

### Remember it
- **AI flashcards**: turn any lesson into spaced-repetition cards.
- **Review page**: one place showing what is due today: flashcards, mistakes, topics.
- **Mistake notebook**: every wrong answer is saved. Sunroot sends you back to re-learn the topic first, then tests you with NEW questions on the same idea (not the same question again).
- **Exam countdown planner**: a revision schedule built backwards from your exam date.
- **Goal tracker**: "Pass physics by March" turned into daily tasks.
- **For you**: a short ranked list of what to study next, with the reason for each; it ends when today's goal is done.
- **My learning map**: how you study, as Sunroot sees it; you can edit it, switch it off or delete it.

### Labs and building
- **Science experiments**: chemistry and physics simulators with sliders and live results.
- **Virtual lab / Maker Studio**: build circuits, robots and 3D designs, test them with simulated physics, no parts needed.
- **Debug my circuit**: photo of your real breadboard in, fixes out.
- **Real-build guide**: a printable shopping list and wiring sheet.
- **Maker Wall and Build contests**: show your build; compete with a shared parts budget.
- **Team builds**: two people edit one design at the same time.
- **Export designs**: printable PDF guide and a 3D-printable file.

### With other people
- **Study groups**: shared notebook, goals and a group streak.
- **Focus rooms**: Pomodoro with friends, silently, with a streak.
- **Anonymous ask**: "I don't get this" without anyone knowing it's you.
- **Peer tutoring**: earn XP for great answers; rate your helpers.
- **Mentor matching**: seniors paired with juniors by subject.
- **Study rooms (lounge)**: drop in and study next to others.
- **Challenges**: quick quiz duels and build challenges.
- **Polls, shout-outs, 60-second student explainers, school communities, live study events, friends and messages.**
- **Weekly leaderboard**: resets every week so newcomers can win; hall of fame.

### Rewards (gold is used only here)
- **XP and levels**, **streaks that count real learning** (30 learning points in a day, not just opening the app), **streak freezes** (earn one and recover a missed day), **badges**, **seasonal quests**, **XP shop** (themes, avatars, titles, outfits for Sprout), **study heatmap** (study days at a glance, like GitHub's).
- Rewards are meant to be forgiving: missing a day does not erase your learning.

### For lecturers and schools
- **Build a class** from a syllabus or notes with AI; **Tidy structure** to fix topic order and prerequisites without using AI.
- **Class heatmap**: see which topics students struggle with, and common misconceptions.
- **AI lesson plans**, **auto-grading** with teacher override, **attendance check-ins**, **announcements** with read receipts.
- **Schools**: school-sponsored student seats, a principal portal and an inter-university cup.
- **Weekly report card** a student can share with a parent or teacher (a link that can be turned off).

### Built for Nigerian phones and data
- **Offline mode**: download lessons and notes for patchy internet.
- **Data-saver mode**: skips audio and 3D unless you tap.
- Works in the browser and as an Android app; 18 colour themes; Sprout can be switched off.

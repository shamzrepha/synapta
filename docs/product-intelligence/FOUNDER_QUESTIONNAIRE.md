# Founder questionnaire

Only questions the code cannot answer. In priority order, each with why it matters. Short answers are fine; "I don't know yet" is a useful answer.

## 1. Decisions that unblock the next 30 days
1. **What should be removed first?** The repo has about five lab systems, two chat systems, two notebook APIs and Worlds beside Classes. Which do real users touch? Why it matters: deleting duplicates is the biggest speed-up and only you know what students use.
2. **What does BILLING_ENFORCED mean in production today?** Is it set? Do you want free users hard-limited, or soft-limited with prompts? Why: it decides whether advertised limits are real and what teachers cost.
3. **Pricing shape.** Are you willing to collapse to Free, Exam Pass and Semester and retire Pro Max, Ultra and the monthly tiers? Why: the research review and the economics both point there.
4. **Who may become a teacher?** Admin approval, school invite, or open? Why: it is the largest access-control hole.
5. **Should AI-built topics reach students before a lecturer reviews them?** Why: today they do.

## 2. Product vision and learning outcomes
6. What should SunRootLearn become in 12 months, in one sentence?
7. Who matters most: the student, the lecturer, or the school paying per seat?
8. What does successful learning mean to you: exam marks, completed topics, retained knowledge after a month? What should a student be able to do after finishing a topic?
9. How should mastery be shown to students and lecturers (a percentage, a colour, "ready for exam")? Why: the mastery engine can be tuned to whichever meaning you choose.
10. What single experience should a student remember?

## 3. Market and users
11. Which universities and departments are the first targets? How many students and lecturers use the app today, and how many are active weekly (please give real numbers or "unknown")?
12. What feedback have users given, and what do they say they would pay for?
13. What are the biggest adoption barriers you have seen (data cost, phone memory, lecturer trust, payment)?
14. Which features have you already promised to users, schools or investors? Why: they cannot be cut.

## 4. Lecturers
15. What is a lecturer's biggest pain point you have heard directly?
16. How much control should lecturers have over AI content before students see it?
17. Would lecturers pay, or should their cost be covered by students or schools? Do any university rules forbid lecturers selling to their own students?

## 5. Money
18. What is the realistic monthly AI budget, and the monthly infrastructure budget?
19. What percentage of students do you expect to pay, and what evidence do you have?
20. What must stay free for students with no money? What must never be monetised?
21. Is the production Gemini key on a paid tier or the free tier? Why: free-tier terms and limits change privacy and capacity.

## 6. Technical strategy
22. What devices must work (lowest phone, memory, Android version)? Is iOS a goal?
23. Which offline features matter most (reading notes, flashcards, past questions)?
24. What is an acceptable AI response time for a tutor answer and for building a lab?
25. Which technical decisions are non-negotiable (Firebase, Next.js, Hetzner, Paystack)?

## 7. Legal and trust
26. Have you taken legal advice on student data, NDPA 2023, school contracts and minors? Are under-18 students allowed?
27. Do you want an AI-use disclosure to lecturers and parents?

## 8. Priorities
28. What are the next three milestones, with dates?
29. Which of these would you be most upset to lose: the reader with Sprout, the labs, the past-question solver, the social feed, schools?
30. What would make you say in six months that this worked?

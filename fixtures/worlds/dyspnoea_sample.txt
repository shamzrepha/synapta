DYSPNOEA
Dyspnoea is the subjective feeling of difficulty in breathing.
CAUSES
Respiratory causes include COPD, asthma and pneumonia.
Cardiac causes include left ventricular failure.
PATHOPHYSIOLOGY
Irritant exposure → chronic inflammation → airway narrowing → air trapping → hypoxaemia
SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS
Orthopnoea is breathlessness when lying flat.
Paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnoea (PND) wakes the patient at night gasping for air.

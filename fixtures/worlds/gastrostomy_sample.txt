GASTROSTOMY FEEDING
DEFINITION
A gastrostomy tube is a tube placed through the abdominal wall into the stomach to give feeds.
PROCEDURE
Do not use the tube for the first 4-6 hours after insertion.
Leave the tube undisturbed for the first 24 hours to allow the stoma tract to form.
Bolus or intermittent feeding
1. Pinch the proximal end of the gastrostomy tube (to prevent air from entering)
2. Attach syringe to end of tube and hang it up to 18 inches above the patient's abdomen
3. Fill up the syringe barrel with feed, and
4. Allow to empty gradually, continue to refill until the prescribed amount is consumed.
NOTE: The barrel should not be allowed to empty completely before refilling to avoid air from entering
COMPLICATIONS
Peristomal infection is the most common complication (5-6?% cases)
If the tube is dislodged place a clean catheter in the stoma immediately to keep the tract open, especially within the first 4 weeks.
NURSING MANAGEMENT
Clean the stoma site 1-2 times daily.
Clean the stoma site daily (three times daily).
